Reconstruction uncertainty from digitized Kaplan-Meier curves

Outcome-specific problems
High priority
Partially addressed
Survival indirect comparisons routinely digitize published Kaplan-Meier curves into pseudo individual patient data and carry the reconstruction downstream as if it were observed, and population-adjusted intervals do not propagate the reconstruction error. Part of the proposed remedy has been built: RESOLVE-IPD (arxiv:2511.01785) generates ensembles of mathematically valid labelings so that reconstruction uncertainty propagates into the downstream meta-analysis, and Rogula et al. (doi:10.1177/23814683221077643) reconstruct from marked censoring times and report curve-fit uncertainty the standard algorithm does not provide. OUT-13 Partially addressed High priority Promising Tractability 3/5

Statement

Survival indirect comparisons routinely digitize published Kaplan-Meier curves into pseudo individual patient data and carry the reconstruction downstream as if it were observed, and population-adjusted intervals do not propagate the reconstruction error. Part of the proposed remedy has been built: RESOLVE-IPD (arxiv:2511.01785) generates ensembles of mathematically valid labelings so that reconstruction uncertainty propagates into the downstream meta-analysis, and Rogula et al. (doi:10.1177/23814683221077643) reconstruct from marked censoring times and report curve-fit uncertainty the standard algorithm does not provide. Multiple reconstruction is therefore no longer merely promising, though it is a valid analogue of multiple imputation only where the repeated reconstructions are proper draws from a calibrated distribution conditional on the image, the risk table and the reported totals, rather than repeated runs of a deterministic algorithm or arbitrary tuning variants. Defining a Bayesian likelihood directly on the reported Kaplan-Meier ordinates and numbers-at-risk table remains unbuilt.

Why it is open

Reconstruction is deterministic given the image, so every standard method runs without complaint and repeating it reveals nothing; the error is silent while the reported interval ignores the extra variability. The published Kaplan-Meier ordinates are dependent derived statistics, so a likelihood defined on them needs an explicit digitization, rounding, censoring and risk-table observation model, which nobody has written. Population-adjusted implementations take reconstructed event and censoring rows straight into the likelihood as data: at commit 9d150e9, cpaic, the unreleased development package on GitHub that is not installable from CRAN, has no observation model for digitization, risk-table interpolation, event allocation or alternative compatible reconstructions. How much this matters depends on image quality and on downstream sensitivity, so it is context-dependent rather than uniformly decisive.

What has been tried

Work What it contributes
Lang, Zhao, Gao and Xu 2025, RESOLVE-IPD: High-Fidelity Individual Patient Data Reconstruction and Uncertainty-Aware Subgroup Meta-Analysis (arxiv:2511.01785) its MAPLE component generates ensembles of mathematically valid labelings, facilitating a propagating meta-analysis that quantifies and reflects uncertainty from subgroup reconstruction, with VEC-KM extracting coordinates and explicit censoring marks from vectorized figures; it is the multiple-imputation analogue the entry proposed
Rogula, Lozano-Ortega and Johnston 2022, A Method for Reconstructing Individual Patient Data From Kaplan-Meier Survival Curves That Incorporate Marked Censoring Times, MDM Policy and Practice reconstruction method using marked censoring times that yields estimates of uncertainty in the curve fit, which the standard Guyot-style algorithm does not provide

Probable solution or research direction

Connect the existing ensemble reconstruction machinery to population-adjusted analysis rather than reinventing it, and state the calibration condition explicitly so the ensemble is a set of draws conditional on the image, risk table and reported totals rather than a set of arbitrary tuning variants. Build the Bayesian likelihood on the reported curve and numbers-at-risk table, which requires an explicit observation model for digitization, rounding, censoring and risk-table reporting. Report how far RMST and extrapolated survival move across reconstructions rather than assuming a uniform inflation, since curve error can partly cancel under integration and can be amplified or damped by the tail model.

Verification

Verdict. Partially addressed. Part of this problem is addressed by existing work; the entry names which part.

The source said. “Survival indirect comparisons routinely digitize published Kaplan-Meier curves to build pseudo individual patient data, then carry that reconstruction into every downstream analysis as if it were observed data, with no propagation of reconstruction uncertainty.”

What was wrong with it. The source listed multiple reconstruction as an unbuilt promising direction; the literature auditor found it implemented in RESOLVE-IPD (arxiv:2511.01785) and partially in Rogula et al. (doi:10.1177/23814683221077643). The code auditor added two qualifications: repeatedly applying a deterministic reconstruction or varying arbitrary tuning choices is not a valid multiple-imputation analogue, and reconstruction error does not monotonically compound, since RMST integrates curve error that can partly cancel while extrapolation can amplify or damp it depending on the tail model.

Literature and prior-art check
partially-solved / supported-with-caveat. Routine practice does treat digitised pseudo-IPD as observed data, and I found no standard tool that propagates reconstruction error, so the description of practice holds. But the proposed direction has begun to be built. Rogula, Lozano-Ortega and Johnston (MDM Policy & Practice 2022, doi 10.1177/23814683221077643) reconstruct IPD using marked censoring times and report that the method yields estimates of the uncertainty in curve fits that traditional methods do not provide. More directly, RESOLVE-IPD (arXiv 2511.01785, Lang, Zhao, Gao and Xu) implements the multiple-reconstruction idea: its MAPLE component ‘generates ensembles of mathematically valid labelings, facilitating a propagating meta-analysis that quantifies and reflects uncertainty from subgroup reconstruction’, with VEC-KM extracting coordinates and explicit censoring marks from vectorised figures to minimise digitisation error. That is the multiple-imputation analogue named in the proposed direction, so it is no longer merely ‘promising’. The Bayesian-likelihood-on-reported-summaries route remains undeveloped; I found nothing implementing it. Cites doi:10.1177/23814683221077643: Reconstruction method that yields estimates of uncertainty in the curve fit, unavailable from the standard Guyot-style algorithm..

How the evidence falls in time

21 papers in the reviewed corpus bear on this problem, published 2011 to 2026. Partial progress is on the record, and later work still calls this open. Both belong in view.

This problem is recurrent: three or more papers spanning at least eight years assert it independently. Sustained restatement by authors who mostly do not cite each other is the strongest evidence this reading can offer that a gap is real rather than one group’s framing.
