Cross-environment invariance as a screen for bridges and modifiers

Adjacent-field methods
High priority
Confirmed open
No invariant-causal-prediction screen for bridge or component assumptions exists in evidence synthesis, so the proposal stands. The nearest published precedent is Tanboga (arXiv:2604.18646), a transport meta-analysis with a precision-weighted sign-stability diagnostic and a two-condition abstention rule that declines to report a pooled estimate when stability is not supported; it operates within a connected evidence base and is a single-author preprint, so it does not certify a bridge across a disconnected gap. ADJ-09 Confirmed open High priority Promising

Statement

No invariant-causal-prediction screen for bridge or component assumptions exists in evidence synthesis, so the proposal stands. The nearest published precedent is Tanboga (arXiv:2604.18646), a transport meta-analysis with a precision-weighted sign-stability diagnostic and a two-condition abstention rule that declines to report a pooled estimate when stability is not supported; it operates within a connected evidence base and is a single-author preprint, so it does not certify a bridge across a disconnected gap. Two qualifications belong in the claim: a trial, era, country or subnetwork is not automatically a valid environment, since that requires harmonized variables, suitable exogenous shifts and adequate within-environment treatment support, and strong instability should flag a bridge for sensitivity analysis rather than trigger automatic rejection, because sampling error, measurement change, case mix and effect-scale non-collapsibility produce instability too.

Why it is open

The bridging assumption that matters concerns the gap where no data exist, so every stability check is necessarily performed somewhere else; passing the check is consistent with the bridge failing exactly where it is used, which makes the procedure able to refute but never to confirm. Invariance methods also need enough distinct environments to separate stable structure from noise, and evidence networks supply very few trials. The environment assumptions themselves, that studies differ in the right way and that an invariant set exists, cannot be verified from the network alone.

What has been tried

Work What it contributes
Tanboga, Stable Transport Meta-Analysis for Heterogeneous Cardiovascular Trials: A Nuisance-Anchor Framework with a Sign-Stability Diagnostic (2026) Nearest published precedent for invariance-style screening with abstention in evidence synthesis: a precision-weighted sign-stability diagnostic with a two-condition abstention rule. Operates within a connected evidence base and says nothing about disconnected-gap bridges.

Probable solution or research direction

Build invariance-based screening that estimates treatment or component effects separately by environment while letting baselines vary, and that flags bridges whose effects are unstable where they can be checked. Establish what counts as a valid environment in a trial network, given harmonization, exogenous shift and treatment support, and characterize how many environments are needed before the signal is usable. Present the output as screening and calibration rather than validation, routing failures into sensitivity analysis rather than into a pass/fail gate.

Verification

Verdict. Confirmed open. Auditors agreed the problem is open and no counterevidence surfaced.

The source said. “Treating each trial, era, country or subnetwork as an environment, one can search for treatment-by-covariate or component-effect structures that remain stable while nuisance baselines vary, and reject or down-weight a candidate bridge whose effect varies strongly where it can be checked. The report labels invariant causal prediction speculative and calls the limitation of invariance-based bridge screening serious.”

What was wrong with it. The source said a candidate bridge should be rejected or down-weighted when its effect varies across environments; GPT-5.6 Sol narrowed that to flagging and sensitivity analysis, because instability has several innocent sources. The literature auditor also named the nearest precedent, arXiv:2604.18646, which the source did not cite.

Literature and prior-art check
open / supported. I found no invariant-causal-prediction screen for bridge or component assumptions in evidence synthesis, so the entry stands. The generic machinery exists (Peters, Buhlmann & Meinshausen’s invariant causal prediction; ‘Active Invariant Causal Prediction: Experiment Selection through Stability’, NeurIPS 2020), and one 2026 preprint does implement a stability screen inside transport meta-analysis: Tanboga, ‘Stable Transport Meta-Analysis for Heterogeneous Cardiovascular Trials: A Nuisance-Anchor Framework with a Sign-Stability Diagnostic’ (arXiv:2604.18646), which ‘incorporates a precision-weighted sign-stability diagnostic with a two-condition abstention rule to avoid reporting a single pooled estimate when stability is not supported’, evaluated in a pre-specified ADEMP simulation. That is the entry’s screen-and-abstain idea, but applied to pooling across connected trials, not to certifying a bridge across a disconnected gap; it is a single-author unrefereed preprint. The entry’s central logical point, that a stability check performed where data exist cannot license the gap where they do not, is correct and is not contradicted by anything I retrieved. Cites arxiv:2604.18646: Does NOT resolve this entry. It is the nearest published precedent for invariance-style screening with abstention in evidence synthesis, and the entry should cite it as such; it operates within a connected evidence base and says nothing about disconnected-gap bridges..

Grok 4.5, recency lens
open / supported. Invariant causal prediction is a real multi-environment method for stable predictors/interactions, but aggressive search did not surface a peer-reviewed ITC/NMA/PAIC implementation that screens component or treatment bridges and is evaluated as operational practice. The report’s core epistemic claims match the method’s known limits: stability can be checked only where environments exist, so a pass never confirms the gap where the bridge is used; few trials supply few environments; environment assumptions are untestable from the network alone. That package of limitations means the problem is still open as stated (speculative screening tool, not validation). No RSM/SIM/MDM/JCE online-first 2024–2026 paper, arXiv stat.ME hit, or package PR closed invariance-based bridge screening for evidence networks.
