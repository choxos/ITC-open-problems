IPD availability may be informative rather than random

Component methods (CNMA and component PAIC)
High priority
Partially addressed
IPD availability may be informative rather than random, and the premise has empirical support: a survey of 31 IPD meta-analyses found that 16 (52 percent) did not obtain all the individual participant data requested, five of those did not mention it as a limitation, and only six examined how the trials without IPD might affect conclusions. Component PAIC conditions on the supplied IPD and provides neither a selection model for availability nor grouped leave-IPD-out validation. Nonrandomness has to be demonstrated or posited as a sensitivity assumption rather than assumed categorically: availability can be modeled where it depends on observed trial-level variables, and only selection on unobserved IPD quantities is unidentified without additional data or assumptions.

CMP-22 Partially addressed High priority Emerging

Statement

IPD availability may be informative rather than random, and the premise has empirical support: a survey of 31 IPD meta-analyses found that 16 (52 percent) did not obtain all the individual participant data requested, five of those did not mention it as a limitation, and only six examined how the trials without IPD might affect conclusions. Component PAIC conditions on the supplied IPD and provides neither a selection model for availability nor grouped leave-IPD-out validation. Nonrandomness has to be demonstrated or posited as a sensitivity assumption rather than assumed categorically: availability can be modeled where it depends on observed trial-level variables, and only selection on unobserved IPD quantities is unidentified without additional data or assumptions.

Why it is open

The IPD edges are the ones that get population-adjusted and the ones that inform interaction parameters, so a selection mechanism on availability propagates into which parts of covariate space are informed by causal evidence rather than by aggregate association. Selection depending on unobserved IPD characteristics cannot be estimated from within the network, because the trials with unobserved IPD are precisely the ones that would identify it. Federated approaches or a full selection model require access or assumptions that are currently unavailable, and no leave-IPD-out facility exists in component PAIC software to at least measure the dependence.

What has been tried

Work What it contributes
Ahmed, Sutton and Riley 2012 surveys 31 IPD meta-analyses and documents that most did not obtain all requested individual participant data and few assessed the impact, establishing that IPD availability is not random
cpaic 0.1.0 (development version, choxos/cpaic@9d150e9) unreleased development R package on GitHub, not on CRAN; conditions on the supplied IPD studies without modeling why IPD are available, and provides observation-level leave-one-out only, with no grouped leave-IPD-out facility

Probable solution or research direction

Implement grouped leave-IPD-out validation first, since it measures how conclusions depend on which trials contributed individual data even though it does not identify or correct the mechanism. Model availability where it depends on observed trial-level characteristics, and treat selection on unobserved characteristics as an explicit sensitivity assumption with a stated selection model rather than as an unmodeled default. Pursue federated methods that let non-sponsor trials contribute individual-level information without releasing data, which is the only route that addresses the cause rather than the symptom.

Verification

Verdict. Partially addressed. Auditors agreed the problem is open and no counterevidence surfaced. Reopened by the full-text reading: 3 findings from 3 papers survived both the reader and an independent second reviewer, so the verdict moves from confirmed-open to partially-addressed.

The source said. “The trials for which IPD is available are typically sponsor-owned and are not a random subset of the evidence base, yet component PAIC treats IPD availability as an exogenous feature of the network.”

What was wrong with it. The source asserted non-random IPD availability categorically and cited nothing. GPT-5.6 Sol flagged the categorical form as unsupported and distinguished selection on observed trial-level variables, which is modelable, from selection on unobserved IPD quantities, which is not identified; the literature auditor supplied the missing empirical source, doi:10.1136/bmj.d7762.

Dissent. literature dissented (supported): The premise is sourceable and the entry sources nothing. Ahmed, Sutton and Riley 2012 surveyed 31 recent IPD meta-analyses of randomised trials and found that ‘Sixteen (52%) of the 31 meta-analyses did not obtain all the individual participant data requested, yet five of these (31%) did not mention this as a potential limitation, and only six (38%) examined how trials without individual participant data might affect the conclusions’, concluding that ‘Publication, availability, and selection biases are a potential concern for meta-analyses of individual participant data’. That converts ‘IPD availability is not random’ from assertion to evidence, and it also shows the field’s partial response - their recommendation is to include aggregate data from the IPD-less studies to gauge impact, which is what a network with mixed IPD and AgD already does. What remains open is exactly what the entry says: a selection model for IPD availability, and leave-IPD-out validation, neither of which I found in the PAIC literature.

How the evidence falls in time

12 papers in the reviewed corpus bear on this problem, published 2012 to 2025. Partial progress is on the record, and later work still calls this open. Both belong in view.

This problem is recurrent: three or more papers spanning at least eight years assert it independently. Sustained restatement by authors who mostly do not cite each other is the strongest evidence this reading can offer that a gap is real rather than one group’s framing.

It is also being closed in pieces: partial results come from more than one paper in more than one year, each covering a different part.
