# DIA-07 design: two networks, one sample size, different estimability

**Status: design. Not registered.** Probes in section 10 not run.
Written against `studies/DESIGN-STANDARD.md`.

**A topology generator exists.** Petropoulou et al. publish a procedure with R code
that builds families of connected and disconnected topologies from a seed network.
It has not been wired to PAIC estimators, and **adapting it is the work rather than
writing another one.**

The catalog narrows two of the entry's own prescriptions and both narrowings change
the design. Information for binary and survival outcomes depends on events **plus
non-events**, allocation, baseline risk, risk sets, censoring and timing, not on
events per contrast alone. And **post-weighting effective sample size is a
method-dependent output**: it can be a calibration target but cannot be crossed as an
independent data-generating factor.

---

## 1. The claim, restated as something that can be false

**Proposition under test:** PAIC simulations are built on two-trial or
three-treatment templates with no topology factor and index information by
participants per arm, so their results cannot be extrapolated to the networks
submissions actually contain; and two networks with identical total sample size can
differ entirely in the estimability of the decision contrast depending on where the
IPD sit.

**Refuting sentence:** *estimator performance depends on topology only through total
information, so results from a two-trial template transfer once total information is
matched.*

## 2. The mechanism: estimability is a row-space property, not an information one

Topology acts through the design matrix $X$, its row space, and the
contrast-specific information matrix. The decision contrast $g$ is estimable if and
only if $g \in \mathrm{rowspace}(X)$, and its precision is $1/[\,I^{-1}]_{gg}$,
which is **zero information when $g$ leaves the row space** and finite otherwise.

Three consequences:

1. **Estimability is discrete and total information is continuous**, so no matching
   of total information can equalize two topologies that differ in whether $g$ is in
   the row space. **The refuting sentence is therefore false in the strongest
   possible way for at least some pairs**, and the design's job is to find how often
   real-shaped networks land there.
2. **Articulation edges are where the discreteness bites.** An articulation edge is
   one whose removal disconnects the graph, so every indirect path through it is the
   only path. **Its influence is not a matter of degree**, which is why the entry
   calls articulation edges and articulation treatments explicit fragility factors,
   and why CMP-24's influence measure matters most there.
3. **Where the IPD sit changes the row space of the *adjusted* design**, not just
   its precision, because an interaction identified only by between-study variation
   is a different coordinate from one identified within study. **IDN-06 owns that
   distinction and this design inherits it**: the same network with IPD moved can
   identify a target-population contrast or not.

**So the design's primary manipulation is placement at fixed total information**,
which is exactly the comparison no existing PAIC simulation makes.

## 3. Estimand, with its true value defined

**Primary.** A prespecified target-population contrast, declared before the
topologies are generated so it is not chosen to be estimable.

**True value** by quadrature over the declared target at an order fixed by P1.

**Estimability is a binary derived estimand with a computed truth**, from the rank
condition in section 2, and **every performance measure is reported conditional on
it**. Averaging bias over replicates where the contrast is not estimable is
meaningless, and P2 computes the map before anything is fitted.

## 4. Data-generating mechanism, and what it makes invisible

**Topologies are generated by the published procedure**, adapted to drive PAIC
estimators.

### Factors

| factor | levels | why |
|---|---|---|
| topology family | chain; star; loop-rich; **with an articulation edge** | consequence 2 |
| IPD placement | on the articulation edge; adjacent to it; distant | **consequence 3, at fixed total information** |
| total outcome information | held fixed across topologies | the design's control, calibrated per outcome type |
| outcome type | binary; survival | the narrowing: information is indexed appropriately for each |
| effect-modifier overlap | good, poor | the adjustment layer |
| target ESS band | calibrated to a range | **a calibration target, not a crossed factor**, per the narrowing |

### What the mechanism makes true, and therefore what the study cannot see

- **Conclusions will be topology-family specific**, which the note says plainly.
  **Performance is reported conditional on topology and never averaged over it**,
  because an average over families is an average over a choice of families.
- Integration points are treated as a **numerical-convergence check** for models
  requiring integration and prior informativeness as a Bayesian sensitivity factor,
  not as mandatory factors, per the narrowing. **Adding them as design factors would
  double the grid to test something that is not about topology.**
- Consistency holds, so topology effects are not confounded with inconsistency;
  HET-04 owns that.
- The generator was written for component model selection. **Whether its topology
  families span what submissions contain is a question P3 asks of the appraisal
  record**, not an assumption.

## 5. Methods

MAIC, STC, ML-NMR and no adjustment, at standard specifications, so the topology
result is not about one estimator.

**The comparator that can win is the two-trial template's own ranking.** If the
method ordering at matched total information is the same across every topology
family, the refuting sentence holds for ranking purposes even though estimability
differs, **and existing simulations transfer better than the entry claims.**
Registered as such.

## 6. Performance measures, MCSE, and $n_{sim}$

**Estimability rate** per topology and placement, from the rank map; and,
**conditional on estimability**, bias, coverage, decision error and convergence,
with MCSE.

**Reported by topology family**, never pooled.

**The registered mechanism check:** whether two networks matched on total
information but differing in placement differ in estimability. **Consequence 1 says
they can; if they never do in realistic families, the sharp version of the claim is
unreachable and the study reports the weaker precision version instead.**

$n_{sim} = 1000$ networks per cell, Stan-limited by the ML-NMR arm.

## 7. Primary outcome and decision rule, before the run

**Primary outcome.** Decision error for the prespecified contrast, by topology
family and IPD placement, at fixed total information.

**Decision rule.**

- Decision error differing materially across placements at fixed total information:
  **confirmed**, and the deliverable is that PAIC simulations must report topology
  and placement, plus the estimability map as a pre-fitting check.
- No material difference: **refuted**, and two-trial templates transfer, which would
  simplify the field's simulation practice considerably.
- **Estimability differences without performance differences** would mean the
  discrete failure is rare and the continuous one is absent, which is a third and
  reassuring result.

## 8. Three controls, each of which can fail

**Null control.** On a two-trial topology, every estimator must reproduce its
behavior in the existing two-trial literature. **A generator study that cannot
reproduce the template it is generalizing has not generalized anything**, and
DIA-08 and DIA-10 register the same control.

**Second null control.** With no effect modification, adjustment is unnecessary and
topology should act only through ordinary network precision, which standard NMA
theory already predicts. **Agreement with that prediction validates the generator
before any adjustment result is interpreted.**

**Positive control.** IPD placed distant from an articulation edge whose contrast is
the target: the contrast must be non-estimable or badly imprecise. **If it is not,
the articulation edge is not doing what section 2 says.**

**Falsifier for the study's own headline.** The expected headline is that topology
matters. Its falsifier is the matched-information control: **if matching total
information equalizes performance across families, then topology matters only
through information and the existing templates need only an information
correction**, which is a far cheaper fix than a topology factor.

## 9. Threats, and what happened to each

| threat | what was done | status |
|---|---|---|
| Writing a new topology generator | The published one is adapted | removed |
| Crossing achieved ESS as an input | Used as a calibration target, per the narrowing | removed |
| Indexing information by participants for binary and survival outcomes | Outcome-appropriate information measures, per the narrowing | removed |
| Averaging over topology families | Reported conditionally, never pooled | removed |
| Bias averaged over non-estimable replicates | Conditional on estimability; map computed first | removed |
| Integration points and priors as mandatory factors | Convergence check and sensitivity factor respectively | removed |
| Generator families not matching real submissions | P3 checks against the appraisal record | removed |

## 10. Probes required before this becomes a protocol

| probe | computes | could change | cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** truth and quadrature order | The target contrast's truth per topology | The definition of truth | hours |
| **P2** estimability map | Rank and contrast-specific precision for every topology and placement, before fitting | **The grid, and the primary outcome's conditioning.** The same probe CMP-14 skipped for four rounds | days |
| **P3** family coverage | Which topologies appear in the appraisal record, so the generated families span practice | Whether the result speaks to submissions | days |
| **P4** information calibration | Outcome-appropriate information matched across topologies, verified numerically | **The design's central control** | days |
| **P5** unit cost | Per-network cost across four estimators; total computed not typed | $n_{sim}$ | hours |

## 11. Cost

Networks times placements times estimators, with ML-NMR fits. **The largest grid in
the queue by network count**, which is why the note orders it after simpler
two-study work. Bounded by SFW-06.
