COV-04 answers this entry's shrinkage and model-averaging route to uncertainty for the whole selection
procedure. With eight candidate interactions in a continuous-outcome STC, 95% posterior intervals for the
transported effect covered 0.794 to 0.948 under an empirical-Bayes ridge prior on the interactions and 0.868
to 0.959 under a hierarchical normal prior, against 0.939 to 0.955 with all interactions unpenalized.
Shrinkage does not select, but when the modifiers the target weights push the effect the same way, their
joint shrinkage biases the transported effect and the narrower interval does not carry that bias.
Spike-and-slab averaging undercovered less (lowest 0.908); conditioning on the median-probability model
behaved like frequentist selection (lowest 0.785).

Not answered: the regularized horseshoe, heredity constraints, binary and survival outcomes, and
interactions of mixed sign relative to the target shift.
