MOD-02 bears on this entry's crossover question in the pairwise case. With a hinge in the treatment effect
and targets 0.5, 1 and 1.5 SD from the source, a GAM with smooth treatment modification had less bias than
parametric STC at every distance (-0.009, -0.017, -0.085 against -0.018, -0.045, -0.144), so there was no
crossover in bias; but its variance grew faster, and at the farthest target the two had the same RMSE (0.314
and 0.318). Under a covariate-by-covariate modification that the additive smooths could not represent, the
flexible model was no better anywhere (bias -0.183 against -0.175 at 1.5 SD). Flexibility therefore helped
only within the shapes it spans, and its gain in the unsupported region was consumed by extrapolation
variance.

Not answered: the network case, BART or Gaussian-process surfaces inside an ML-NMR likelihood, and the
computational burden the entry names; these results are from a single individual-data trial.
