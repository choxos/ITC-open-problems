**Study `studies/MIS-03-target-moment-uncertainty`, run and decided against a rule registered
before the run.** 252 scenarios, 5000 replicates each.

**Conclusion: uninformative, and the study says so rather than reporting its estimates.** The
convergence and bias gates passed, but the reference validity and negative-control gates
**failed**, so the comparison the study was built to make cannot be read off it. The estimates
exist; the prespecified rule refuses to interpret them, which is the outcome a registered
decision rule exists to make available.

A successor study on non-collapsible scales, `studies/EST-07-moment-variance-noncollapsible`,
is designed and under review but has not been run.
